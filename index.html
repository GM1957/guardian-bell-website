How it works

A check-in, end to end.

From the first ring to the day’s verdict, here’s exactly what happens.

It rings, like an alarm

At check-in time Guardian Bell rings with your alarm tone and opens its own incoming check-in screen. Answer it, or decline and say when to come back.

  • Exact-time alarms that work with the app closed and survive a restart
  • Rings again every 5 minutes until you answer or decline
  • Declining asks when to remind you, and why

Hear the question. Answer once.

A short pre-recorded prompt plays and appears on screen. Your open commitments are listed underneath. Then you answer in one box.

  • Speak for as long as you need: it listens until you tap stop
  • Or type, or mix both
  • No back-and-forth chat to sit through

AI turns your answer into a plan

Your AI provider reads the answer and records what it means: new commitments with deadlines, what’s done, what slipped and why, and when to check in next.

  • Shows exactly what it recorded, nothing hidden
  • Picks the next check-in around your next action
  • Never schedules sooner than your minimum interval

Your day at a glance

The Today screen shows your mission, how much is done, the next check-in, and every commitment with its deadline.

  • “Enter your first check-in” until today’s plan is set
  • The check-in button lights up the moment one is due or owed
  • Emergency / Unavailable pauses everything, without penalties

An honest verdict every day

Each day ends as pass, fail or incomplete, decided by the app from what happened. The report explains how the day went and what to change tomorrow.

  • Pass/fail is computed from facts, never by the AI
  • Hard truth and tomorrow’s correction
  • Your own rating and reflection, by voice or text
Strict, but fair

Hard on excuses. Never unfair.

Accountability only works if the record is trustworthy, so the rules are written into the app, not left to the AI.

15 min

Grace on every deadline

A deadline only counts as overdue 15 minutes after it passes.

Locked

Time locked at check-in start

Answers are judged as of the moment you opened the check-in, so taking time to answer never turns a pass into a fail.

Pending

Postponed isn’t failed

Decline and ask to be reminded later: overdue work shows as awaiting check-in until you report on it.

Facts

Pass/fail by the app

The result comes from whether critical commitments were met. The AI can’t move the goalposts.

Paused

Real life is excused

Approved pauses and outside interruptions never count as failures, and history is never rewritten.

Min.

Your interval is a hard limit

Routine check-ins never come sooner than your minimum interval, and only within your active hours.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Do I need an AI provider?

Yes. Guardian Bell uses AI to read your check-in answers and write your reports. You choose the provider: Claude, Gemini, Groq, OpenRouter, OpenAI, Mistral, or your own server such as Ollama or LM Studio. Several have free tiers.

What happens if I decline a check-in?

You pick when to be reminded (20 minutes, 1 hour, 2 hours, or 30 minutes by default) and can add a reason. The check-in stays pending, not failed, and you can check in any time before the reminder.

Will I fail a day because I was slow to answer?

No. Deadlines have a 15-minute grace period, and your answer is judged as of the moment you opened the check-in, not when you pressed submit. A deadline that passes while a check-in is postponed shows as awaiting check-in until you report on it.

Who decides pass or fail?

The app, from stored facts: whether each critical commitment was met by its agreed deadline. The AI writes the narrative but never changes the result. Approved pauses and outside interruptions never count as failures.

Where is my data stored?

On your phone. Check-ins, commitments and reports are kept in the app’s local database, and API keys in the phone’s secure storage. Your answers are sent only to the AI provider you choose.

Does it work on iPhone?

Guardian Bell is built for Android and iOS. Alarm-style ringing over the lock screen and measured app usage are Android features; on iPhone, check-in reminders arrive as notifications.
