Why it's different

Productivity apps organize your tasks. Guardian Bell protects your why.

Lists and planners assume you'll stay motivated. You won't always. Guardian Bell is built for the moment motivation disappears: it interrupts the drift, reminds you what you committed to, and asks you to answer for it out loud.

Typical productivity apps

  • Wait for you to open them
  • Track what you planned to do
  • Checkboxes you can quietly ignore
  • Notifications you swipe away

Guardian Bell

  • Calls you, so you have to respond
  • Asks what you actually got done
  • Your spoken answer becomes a record you can revisit
  • Streaks that show whether you're showing up
How it works

Four steps that make follow-through automatic.

Set it once at the start of a focus session. Guardian Bell takes care of keeping you honest.

  1. Name your focus

    Write the one thing you're committing to today, like "Ship the first draft".

  2. Pick your rhythm

    Choose how often your accountability partner calls, such as every 25 minutes.

  3. Answer the call

    Say what you got done, what's next, and what's in your way. Your words are transcribed as you speak.

  4. Review and keep the streak

    Replay reflections, read transcripts, and watch your daily streak grow.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is Guardian Bell a productivity app?

It sits in the productivity category, but it is built for personal accountability. Instead of organizing tasks, it checks in on you and asks whether you are still working on what matters.

Where are my voice reflections stored?

On your phone. Each reflection is saved as an audio file plus a text transcript in the app's private storage. There is no account and no cloud upload.

How does transcription work?

Guardian Bell uses your phone's built-in speech recognition. On iOS it runs on-device when available; on Android it uses the system recognizer, which may use the network. Transcription needs Android 13+ or iOS.

Does it call me when the app is closed?

Not yet. Check-in calls currently ring while the app is open, and the screen stays awake during a focus session. Background calls are on the roadmap.

When can I download it?

Guardian Bell is in development for Android and iOS. Store listings are coming soon.
